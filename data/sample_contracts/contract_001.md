## 2. Services

Cedar Finch will design, configure, and support a document analytics platform
for Northstar. The services include a secure ingestion API, document parsing,
search indexing, dashboard configuration, data-quality monitoring, and a
monthly operational review. Cedar Finch will provide a delivery manager,
platform engineer, data engineer, and security contact. The initial statement
of work is **Statement of Work A**, attached conceptually to this sample and
incorporated only for testing purposes.

The implementation will use a development environment, a staging environment,
and a production environment. Cedar Finch will not move a document from
staging to production without written approval from Northstar’s project owner,
**Mira Solberg**. Northstar’s technical owner is **Jonah Reyes**.

## 3. Deliverables and acceptance

Cedar Finch will deliver an architecture outline, an ingestion workflow, a
metadata dictionary, a search evaluation report, runbooks, and a production
readiness checklist. Each deliverable will be submitted through the shared
project workspace. Northstar will have ten business days to review a
deliverable. A deliverable is accepted when Northstar confirms acceptance in
writing or does not provide a written rejection describing a material defect
within that review period.

If Northstar rejects a deliverable in good faith, Cedar Finch will correct the
identified material defect within a reasonable period agreed by the Parties.
Changes to scope, source systems, retention requirements, or acceptance tests
will be recorded in a written change order signed by both project owners.

## 4. Fees and payment

Northstar will pay Cedar Finch a fictional implementation fee of **USD 48,500**
and a fictional support fee of **USD 6,200 per month** during the first twelve
months. Cedar Finch will invoice monthly in arrears. Northstar will pay each
undisputed invoice within thirty calendar days after receipt. Any invoice
dispute must identify the disputed line item and be raised within fifteen days.

Neither Party may charge expenses without prior written approval. Approved
travel will be reimbursed at cost with receipts. Taxes imposed on Cedar Finch’s
income remain Cedar Finch’s responsibility; transaction taxes that Northstar is
legally required to pay will be handled according to applicable law.

## 5. Data handling and security

Northstar remains the owner of all documents and business information supplied
to Cedar Finch. Cedar Finch may process that information only to provide the
services and may not sell, advertise with, or use it to train a general model.
Access will be limited to personnel who need access for the services and who
are bound by confidentiality obligations.

Cedar Finch will use encryption in transit and at rest, unique user accounts,
multi-factor authentication for administrative access, least-privilege access
roles, and audit logging. Cedar Finch will maintain a written incident response
procedure. If Cedar Finch discovers unauthorized access to Northstar data, it
will notify Northstar without unreasonable delay and provide known facts,
containment steps, and a contact for coordination. Cedar Finch will not include
secrets, passwords, or private keys in logs or test fixtures.

Northstar may request a summary of Cedar Finch’s security controls once each
year. Security requests must be reasonable and must not expose another
customer’s confidential information.

## 6. Confidentiality

Each Party may receive non-public technical, commercial, security, or business
information from the other Party. The receiving Party will use that information
only for this Agreement, protect it with reasonable care, and disclose it only
to personnel or advisers who need it and are bound to confidentiality. The
obligations do not apply to information that is publicly available without
breach, already known without restriction, independently developed, or received
lawfully from a third party.

If disclosure is required by law, the receiving Party will provide advance
notice when legally permitted and disclose only what is required. Confidentiality
obligations survive for five years after termination, except that trade secrets
remain protected while they qualify as trade secrets.

## 7. Intellectual property

Northstar owns its documents, data, trademarks, and pre-existing materials.
Cedar Finch owns its pre-existing tools, generalized know-how, and reusable
software components. Upon full payment, Northstar receives a perpetual,
non-exclusive license to use the custom configuration and documentation
delivered specifically for Northstar. Cedar Finch will not incorporate a
third-party component into a deliverable in a way that prevents Northstar from
using the deliverable as described in the applicable statement of work.

## 8. Term and termination

This Agreement begins on the Effective Date and continues for twenty-four
months unless terminated earlier. Either Party may terminate for convenience
with sixty days’ written notice. Either Party may terminate for material breach
if the breach is not cured within thirty days after written notice. A Party may
terminate immediately if the other Party becomes insolvent or ceases ordinary
business operations.

On termination, Cedar Finch will stop processing new Northstar documents,
return or securely delete Northstar data as directed, and provide reasonable
transition assistance for up to thirty days at the agreed support rate.

Neither Party is liable for indirect, incidental, special, or consequential
losses. Each Party’s aggregate liability is limited to fees paid or payable in
