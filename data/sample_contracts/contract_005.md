## 2. License and implementation

Pixel Harbor grants Juniper Arc a non-exclusive, non-transferable license to use
the hosted workflow platform for Juniper Arc’s internal editorial operations
during the subscription term. The platform includes role management, approval
queues, asset metadata, search, audit history, and export tools. Juniper Arc may
permit contractors to use the platform only for Juniper Arc work and remains
responsible for their access.

Pixel Harbor will configure three workspaces, import up to 120,000 synthetic
asset records, create five approval workflows, and deliver two training
sessions. Juniper Arc will provide a source-field map, sample records, branding
assets, and timely decisions. The Parties will record assumptions and rejected
mapping decisions in the implementation log.

## 3. Content and usage restrictions

Juniper Arc retains ownership of its articles, images, audio, marks, metadata,
and editorial instructions. Juniper Arc represents that it has the rights needed
to provide content for the service. Pixel Harbor may process content only to
host, configure, support, and improve the contracted service for Juniper Arc.

Pixel Harbor may not publish, sell, train a public model on, or use Juniper Arc
content to create a competing customer deliverable. Pixel Harbor may use
irreversibly aggregated performance metrics that do not reveal content, users,
campaigns, or confidential business information.

## 4. Security and privacy

Pixel Harbor will use administrative multi-factor authentication, role-based
permissions, encrypted transport, encrypted storage, backup controls, and
security logging. It will review privileged access quarterly and remove access
when staff leave the engagement. Pixel Harbor will maintain a vulnerability
management process and prioritize critical issues for prompt remediation.

Juniper Arc will not upload passwords, API secrets, private keys, or sensitive
personal records into a test workspace. If production content is later approved,
the Parties will document retention, deletion, access, and incident obligations
in a written addendum. Pixel Harbor will notify Juniper Arc of a suspected
security incident without unreasonable delay and will cooperate on containment.

Support logs must use ticket identifiers and redacted excerpts. Neither Party
will place credentials in plain text inside a ticket, export, filename, or
document metadata field.

## 5. Acceptance, fees, and support

Juniper Arc will review the configured workflows within ten business days after
delivery. Acceptance occurs when the workflows satisfy the agreed test cases or
when Juniper Arc does not provide a written material defect list during the
review period. Pixel Harbor will correct material defects without a separate
fee. New workflows, custom integrations, or additional migrations require a
change order.

The fictional implementation fee is **USD 31,750**, payable forty percent at
start, forty percent at configuration acceptance, and twenty percent at final
handover. The subscription fee is **USD 2,900 per month** for the initial term.
Invoices are payable within thirty days. Approved out-of-scope work is billed
at a fictional rate of **USD 185 per hour**.

Pixel Harbor will provide business-day support. Severity-one incidents affecting
all users will receive an acknowledgement within one hour. Severity-two
incidents will receive an acknowledgement within four hours. Planned maintenance
will be announced at least three business days in advance when practical.

## 6. Confidentiality and intellectual property

Each Party will protect the other Party’s non-public technical, financial,
creative, and business information. Disclosure is limited to personnel,
professional advisers, and approved subcontractors with a need to know. The
obligation does not cover public information, independently developed
information, prior knowledge without restriction, or lawful third-party receipt.

Pixel Harbor owns the hosted platform, generic connectors, implementation
patterns, and pre-existing tools. Juniper Arc owns its content and brand assets.
Custom workflow definitions and documentation paid for by Juniper Arc may be
used by Juniper Arc internally during and after the term. Third-party licenses
remain subject to their own terms.

## 7. Termination and exit

The initial subscription term is twelve months. Either Party may terminate for
convenience with sixty days’ notice after the first six months. Either Party may
terminate for material breach after thirty days to cure. Juniper Arc may suspend
uploads if Pixel Harbor reports an unresolved security incident.

At termination, Pixel Harbor will provide an export of Juniper Arc’s content and
metadata in an agreed format and offer up to thirty days of transition support.
After confirmation of export, Pixel Harbor will delete active customer copies
within forty-five days, subject to backup expiration and legal retention
requirements. Pixel Harbor will not retain a usable copy for product training.
