# SYNTHETIC TEST DATA — RESEARCH DATA LICENSE AND COLLABORATION AGREEMENT

**Fictional document. Not legal advice. Not a real contract.**

**Agreement ID:** DG-SAMPLE-003
**Effective Date:** March 18, 2026

## 1. Parties

This fictional Research Data License and Collaboration Agreement is between
**Silver Fern Biologics, Inc.**, a fictional Oregon corporation at 2600 Cedar
Quarry Drive, Portland, Oregon 97205 (“Silver Fern”), and **Quillstone Research
Institute**, a fictional nonprofit institute at 14 Observatory Green, Boulder,
Colorado 80302 (“Quillstone”). Silver Fern and Quillstone are collaborating on
a synthetic research archive and document retrieval evaluation. No scientific
result, organization, researcher, dataset, or obligation in this document is
real.

The project leads are **Priya Anand** for Silver Fern and **Caleb Hart** for
Quillstone. The project will compare document retrieval quality, entity
normalization, relationship discovery, and citation completeness using invented
or de-identified records.

## 2. License grant and permitted use

Silver Fern grants Quillstone a limited, non-exclusive, non-transferable license
to access the project dataset during the term. Quillstone may load the dataset
into an isolated research environment, create derived indexes, and produce
evaluation metrics. Quillstone may not sell the dataset, identify individuals,
combine it with external personal profiles, reverse engineer restricted source
systems, or use it to make decisions about a real person.

Quillstone grants Silver Fern a reciprocal license to review project code,
evaluation reports, aggregate metrics, and non-sensitive documentation created
under the project. Neither Party receives ownership of the other Party’s
pre-existing tools, methods, or materials.

## 3. Data governance

Each Party will maintain a data inventory identifying source, classification,
owner, retention period, and permitted users. The project dataset must not
contain direct identifiers, medical records, financial account numbers, secret
credentials, or content that a Party is not authorized to share. If a Party
discovers prohibited content, it will stop processing the affected item,
restrict access, notify the other Party, and securely remove or quarantine the
item.

The Parties will use unique accounts, multi-factor authentication, encrypted
storage, encrypted transport, access reviews every ninety days, and separate
research and administrative roles. Quillstone will keep access records for at
least twelve months. Research notebooks must reference synthetic record IDs
rather than copying sensitive source text.

## 4. Publication and review

Quillstone may publish aggregate evaluation results after giving Silver Fern
thirty days to review a draft for confidential information, accidental
identification, or disclosure of security-sensitive details. Silver Fern may
request removal of its confidential information, but may not suppress an honest
scientific conclusion solely because the result is unfavorable.

Neither Party may use the other Party’s name, logo, or endorsement without
written permission. A publication will state that the dataset and organizations
described in this sample agreement are fictional where this sample text is used
for software testing.

## 5. Milestones and funding

The fictional project has four milestones: dataset validation by April 10,
baseline retrieval by May 1, hybrid graph evaluation by June 5, and final report
by July 15, 2026. Silver Fern will provide a fictional research contribution of
**USD 21,400** in two installments. Quillstone will contribute staff time and
will not incur reimbursable expenses without written approval from Priya Anand.

Each milestone requires a short written record describing completed work,
known limitations, and open risks. A missed milestone will trigger a planning
meeting rather than an automatic acceptance or payment reduction.

## 6. Confidentiality and intellectual property

Confidential information includes unpublished research plans, dataset schemas,
security findings, and non-public evaluation results. The receiving Party will
protect it with reasonable safeguards and disclose it only to authorized
personnel or advisers. Public information, independently developed information,
and information lawfully received without restriction are excluded.

Silver Fern owns its source materials and domain knowledge. Quillstone owns its
pre-existing research methods and general improvements that do not disclose
Silver Fern confidential information. Project-specific reports are jointly
available to both Parties for internal use. No Party may train a public model
using the dataset or derived records without a separate written license.

## 7. Term, suspension, and deletion

The term is twelve months from the Effective Date. Either Party may terminate
for convenience with thirty days’ notice. A Party may suspend access immediately
if it reasonably believes the dataset is being misused or a security control
has failed. The other Party will receive notice and a chance to provide a
remediation plan.

Within thirty days after termination, Quillstone will export agreed aggregate
results, delete working copies, revoke project credentials, and provide a
written deletion statement. One encrypted archival copy may be retained only
if required by institutional policy and must remain inaccessible to ordinary
research users.

## 8. Representations and general terms

Each Party represents that it has authority to participate and will follow
applicable research, privacy, and security requirements. The project is not
medical treatment, clinical validation, or regulatory approval. The Parties do
not guarantee a particular research result. Neither Party is liable for
indirect losses; direct liability is capped at the fictional contribution paid
under this Agreement, except for fraud, willful misconduct, or intentional
misuse of confidential information.

Colorado law governs this fictional agreement. Disputes will first be reviewed
by Priya Anand and Caleb Hart. The Parties are independent collaborators, and
this document does not create a partnership, agency, or employment relationship.
