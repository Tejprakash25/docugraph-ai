**Effective Date:** April 6, 2026

## 1. Parties and commercial purpose

This fictional Logistics Procurement and Supplier Agreement is between **Cobalt
River Outfitters, LLC**, a fictional Colorado company at 515 Mesa Current Road,
Fort Collins, Colorado 80524 (“Cobalt River”), and **Marble Kite Components
Co.**, a fictional Nevada company at 2300 Silver Basin Parkway, Reno, Nevada
89502 (“Marble Kite”). The agreement governs the fictional supply of tracking
labels, handheld scanners, and replacement batteries. It exists only as test
content for DocuGraph AI.

The commercial contacts are **Sonia Bell** for Cobalt River and **Marcus Vale**
for Marble Kite. Cobalt River may issue purchase orders under this agreement.
An accepted purchase order identifies the item, quantity, price, delivery
location, requested date, and any approved specification.

## 2. Products and quality

Marble Kite will supply products that conform to the applicable purchase order,
approved samples, and the written specifications in the product schedule. Each
shipment must include a packing list, batch identifier, quantity, and country
of manufacture. Marble Kite will notify Cobalt River before substituting a
component, changing a manufacturing location, or materially changing a process.

Cobalt River may inspect a shipment within fifteen business days. Notice of a
defect does not waive rights for hidden defects discovered later. Marble Kite
will, at Cobalt River’s option, repair, replace, or refund a defective product
within a reasonable period. Repeated quality failures may trigger a corrective
action plan and a temporary suspension of new purchase orders.

## 3. Forecasts, orders, and delivery

Cobalt River may provide a rolling six-month forecast. Forecasts are planning
estimates and are not binding unless converted into an accepted purchase order.
Marble Kite will confirm or reject an order within five business days. The
fictional standard lead time is twenty-one days after confirmation. Partial
shipments require Cobalt River’s approval.

Risk of loss transfers after delivery and inspection at the named location.
Marble Kite will promptly notify Sonia Bell of delays, shortages, or events
likely to affect delivery. If a delay exceeds ten business days, Cobalt River
may source the affected quantity elsewhere and recover the reasonable difference
between the agreed price and replacement price, subject to the liability terms.

## 4. Pricing and payment

Prices are stated in the accepted purchase order and remain fixed for ninety
days unless the order says otherwise. The initial fictional program value is
**USD 86,900**. Marble Kite will invoice after shipment, and Cobalt River will
pay undisputed invoices within forty-five days. Cobalt River may withhold a
disputed amount while the Parties investigate, but will pay undisputed amounts
on time.

No supplier surcharge, tooling fee, or expedited freight charge is payable
without written approval. Taxes, duties, and shipping responsibilities will be
specified in each purchase order. Marble Kite will keep accurate order and
quality records for three years.

## 5. Responsible sourcing and security

Marble Kite will maintain reasonable workplace, environmental, and supply-chain
controls. It will not knowingly use forced labor, child labor, or materials
that violate applicable trade restrictions. It will notify Cobalt River of a
material compliance concern affecting products supplied under this agreement.

If the Parties exchange order files electronically, each will use authenticated
accounts, least-privilege access, encrypted transfer, and approved storage.
Marble Kite will not place passwords, access tokens, or private keys in packing
lists, support tickets, or test data. A suspected unauthorized disclosure of
order information must be reported to the other Party without unreasonable
delay with available containment information.

## 6. Intellectual property and confidentiality

Each Party owns its pre-existing designs, marks, and procedures. Cobalt River
owns its purchase forecasts and internal logistics information. Marble Kite
owns its manufacturing methods and product designs, while Cobalt River receives
the rights necessary to use purchased products and related documentation.

Confidential information may be used only to perform this agreement and may be
disclosed to personnel or subcontractors with a need to know. Information that
is public, independently developed, already known without restriction, or
lawfully received from a third party is not confidential under this section.

## 7. Term, warranty, and termination

The initial term is two years. Either Party may terminate for convenience with
sixty days’ notice, but accepted purchase orders remain in effect unless the
notice addresses them specifically. Either Party may terminate for material
breach after thirty days to cure. Cobalt River may terminate immediately for a
serious product-safety concern, deliberate misconduct, or unlawful sourcing.

Marble Kite warrants that products will be new, conforming, and free from
material defects for twelve months after delivery. Neither Party gives implied
warranties beyond those that cannot be excluded by law. Neither Party is liable
for indirect losses. Direct liability is capped at amounts paid for the affected
products during the prior twelve months, except for fraud, intentional misuse,
confidentiality breach, or indemnity obligations.

## 8. General terms

The Parties are independent contractors. Marble Kite may use a subcontractor
only if it remains responsible for that subcontractor’s work. Assignment
requires consent except for a qualifying corporate reorganization. Nevada law
governs this fictional agreement. Commercial disputes will be escalated to
Sonia Bell and Marcus Vale before formal proceedings. Written purchase orders,
amendments, and signed change orders control over inconsistent summaries.
