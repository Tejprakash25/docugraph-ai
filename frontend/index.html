Frequently asked questions

Can individuals sign up?

No. DocugraphAI is available to organizations only, by invitation.

What data do you access?

Only the documents your organization uploads. Nothing else.

Where does my data go?

Documents are stored on your dedicated deployment. In Pilot and Team, small relevant text snippets are sent to our AI providers to generate answers. In Private, the AI runs on your servers and nothing leaves.

What happens after the pilot?

If the agreed success criteria are met, you move to Team. If not, you stop with no further charges.

What if we exceed our allowance?

We notify you first. Extra usage is billed at the rate in your agreement, or you can upgrade.

Do prices include GST?

No. 18% GST is added to all prices.
